🪙 an obol — the ferryman’s coin

In the old story the dead were buried with a coin under the tongue: the fare to cross. No coin, no crossing — you wait on the shore.

Charon mints each soul’s obols from cheap, honest signals. Start 5.  +2 brings tests · +1 a real account (not a one-line wish) · +1 a typed title · +1 it cites the issues it answers · −2 still a draft · −1 one of a same-shape swarm. Clamped 0–10 — 7 or more climbs aboard.

6 or 7? …even the ferryman can’t always tell. 🪙
